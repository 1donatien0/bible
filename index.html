The app

Sainte Bible is a free iPhone, iPad and Android app for reading the Bible in the Louis Segond 1910 French translation. The whole text is included in the app: it works without an Internet connection.

Features

  • 66 booksOld and New Testament, 31,102 verses
  • SearchAcross the whole Bible, accents optional
  • Verse of the dayOn the home screen and as a notification
  • HighlightingFour colours, on one or several verses
  • Favourites and notesYour verses and your personal thoughts
  • OfflineNo connection needed, no data collected

Privacy

The app collects no personal information and sends nothing to third-party servers. Your favourites, notes and highlights stay on your device. Details in the privacy policy.
